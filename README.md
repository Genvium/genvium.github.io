# Genvium

**Funding Made Simple for Startups**

Genvium is a desktop platform that helps startups discover, organize, and track funding opportunities in one place. Instead of searching across dozens of websites, founders can use Genvium to quickly find grants, startup competitions, accelerators, venture funding, and other financing opportunities tailored to their business.

With powerful search and filtering tools, direct application links, and built-in tracking features, Genvium streamlines the funding process from discovery to submission.

---

## Features

- Centralized database of startup funding opportunities
- Advanced filtering and search capabilities
- Discover:
  - Grants
  - Startup competitions
  - Accelerators
  - Venture capital opportunities
  - Incubators
- Direct links to external application pages
- Track submissions, deadlines, and application progress
- Desktop-first experience focused on productivity
- Designed for early-stage startups and scaling teams

---

## Why Genvium?

Finding startup funding is fragmented and time-consuming. Genvium simplifies the process by bringing opportunities and workflow management into a single platform so founders can spend less time searching and more time building.

## Getting Started

1. Install and launch Genvium
2. Create or sign in to your account
3. Browse funding opportunities
4. Apply using direct external links
5. Track deadlines and application progress from your dashboard
